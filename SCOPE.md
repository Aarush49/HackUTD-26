# Product scope

Canopy is a finance app with one dashboard of everything, plus headline impact, goals, a loss predictor, and a long-range simulator.

## Keep

- Dashboard: Picture contains the complete account view; Today is a short summary.
- Headline impact: translates headlines into estimated effects on income, rates, and goal timing.
- Goals: shows ETA, progress, and option comparisons with benefits and tradeoffs.
- Predictor: ranks where the user might lose money, why, the estimated range, and a suggested action.
- Simulator: compares two or three long-run plans with adjustable monthly amount, years, and rate.
- Garden, authentication and onboarding, headline-only What If sheet, established visual system, trust labels, and typed mock data.

## Remove

- Breaking-point timers and related fields or UI.
- Hypothetical financial stress presets, drain-order visualizations, and their mode.
- Stress-driven garden behavior and the stress option in What If.
- Thirty-day cash timeline and repair-action success UI.
- External bank sandbox integration, notification experiments, chat or voice assistants, bank-side consoles, and unrelated prototypes.

Features not classified above must remain unchanged until they are explicitly classified.
